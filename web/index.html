Fly the rocket yourself, in three dimensions. Real two-body orbital mechanics with inclination, staging, atmospheric flight and a planet you can actually fly around. Best held sideways.

Select a vehicle

Getting to orbit

Climb to about 2 km, then pitch east — that is toward the horizon, away from the planet's rotation axis — and follow your own prograde marker down to the horizon by 45 km.

Watch apoapsis, not altitude. Past 80 km, cut the throttle and coast.

At apoapsis burn horizontally until periapsis clears 70 km. That is orbit.

Drag the background to swing the camera around the rocket; pinch or scroll to zoom. View cycles chase, cockpit and wide.
