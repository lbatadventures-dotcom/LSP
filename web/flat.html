Getting to orbit

Climb to about 2 km, then tip east and let the rocket follow its own prograde marker down toward the horizon by 45 km. Fighting the airflow bleeds speed and can flip you.

Watch apoapsis, not altitude. When it passes 80 km, cut the throttle and coast.

At apoapsis, burn sideways until periapsis climbs above 70 km. That is orbit — the low point has to clear the atmosphere, not just the high point.

Time warp only engages outside the atmosphere with the engines off.
